LibreUSOS is an unofficial, free and open-source client for USOS, the university system used at Polish universities. It currently targets the Silesian University of Technology (PolSl).

Features:
* Sign in through your browser with USOS OAuth; your password never reaches the app
* Today: your classes for the day at a glance
* Timetable with a week pager and pull-to-refresh
* Grades per term, with averages when USOS provides them
* Tests (sprawdziany): points and results from your courses
* Home-screen widget with the timetable
* Background sync every 6 hours, with cached data shown first so the app also works offline

No ads, no trackers, no Google services and no proprietary dependencies. The core is written in Rust and shared with other LibreUSOS clients; the app uses Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

LibreUSOS is not affiliated with or endorsed by any university or the USOS developers.
